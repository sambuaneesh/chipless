<p>Got a deck of cards but no poker chips? <b>Chipless</b> turns one phone into the chips, the pot, the dealer button and a patient rules coach for real-card <b>No-Limit Texas Hold'em</b> home games.</p>

<p><b>Two ways to play</b></p>
<ul>
<li><b>Pass and play:</b> one phone in the middle of the table runs everything.</li>
<li><b>Host a table:</b> your phone serves the game over Wi-Fi or its own hotspot. Friends scan a QR code and play from their phone's browser. They don't install anything, and no internet connection is needed.</li>
</ul>

<p><b>Runs the hand for you</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Posts the blinds, moves the button and tells the dealer what to deal next.</li>
<li>Shows whose turn it is and the exact amount to call or raise.</li>
<li>Every bet, call and fold pops up big on every phone, with a timeline of the round. Optional dramatic sounds and spoken bets.</li>
<li>Optionally record the flop, turn and river so everyone sees the board.</li>
<li>Drag to rearrange seats.</li>
<li>Handles all-ins, side pots, minimum raises and split pots.</li>
<li>Undo for any mistake, and the game is saved after every action.</li>
</ul>

<p><b>Teaches beginners as you play</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Plain-language hints that fade once you know the rules.</li>
<li>"Who wins?" showdown helper: enter the cards to see the winning hand and why it wins.</li>
<li>Hand rankings always one tap away.</li>
</ul>

<p><b>Free play or real buy-ins</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Play for points with a leaderboard, or track real buy-ins and rebuys.</li>
<li>At the end of the night, settle up with the fewest possible payments.</li>
<li>Export a readable hand history, or back up the game and continue it on another phone.</li>
<li>Optional blind timer for tournament-style nights.</li>
</ul>

<p><b>Small, fast and private</b></p>
<ul>
<li>About 125 KB. No ads, no accounts, no analytics, no cloud.</li>
<li>Works offline in the same room. The table never leaves your local network.</li>
<li>Free and open source (GPL-3.0).</li>
</ul>

<p>Chipless never deals cards: you play with a real deck, it keeps track of everything else.</p>
