Poker settle-up calculator

End of the night and nobody knows who owes whom? Enter what each player paid in and what they left with. You'll get everyone's result and the fewest payments that settle it.

Who pays whom

Amounts in money, not chips. "Bought in" is the total of every buy-in and rebuy. "Cashed out" is what their final chips are worth.

How it works

  1. Net result. Each player's result is what they cashed out minus what they bought in. Winners are positive, losers negative.
  2. Balance check. The money that came in must equal the money that went out. If it doesn't, a stack was miscounted or a rebuy wasn't written down. Fix that before anyone pays.
  3. Fewest payments. The calculator looks for groups of players whose results cancel out exactly and settles each group on its own. That gives the smallest possible number of payments, not just a good guess.

Example

Four friends play with buy-ins of 500. Maya leaves with 1,200, Arjun busts after one buy-in, Sam rebuys once and leaves with 600, and Zoe leaves with 700.

PlayerBought inCashed outNet
Maya5001,200+700
Arjun5000−500
Sam1,000600−400
Zoe500700+200

Total in: 2,500. Total out: 2,500, so it balances. Three payments settle it: Arjun pays Maya 500, Sam pays Maya 200, and Sam pays Zoe 200.

Two ways to settle

Direct payments (fewest transfers)

Losers pay winners directly. This is what the calculator above works out, and it's best when everyone pays by phone.

Through a banker

One person collects all the cash at the start and pays winners at the end. It takes more payments but is simpler with cash, because the banker holds the money from the first buy-in.

Questions

What if the totals don't match?

Recount the final stacks and check every rebuy was recorded. A small difference usually means someone's chips were counted wrong. Agree on a fix at the table before anyone pays.

How do I convert chips to money?

Divide the buy-in amount by the number of chips each buy-in gets. If a 500 buy-in gets 1,000 chips, each chip is worth 0.50, so a final stack of 2,400 chips is worth 1,200.

Is my data saved or sent anywhere?

No. The calculator runs entirely in your browser; nothing you type leaves your device.
