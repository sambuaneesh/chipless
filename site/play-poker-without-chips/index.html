How to play poker without chips

You have a deck of cards and some friends, but no chip set. Here's how to run a proper Texas Hold'em game anyway: what to use instead of chips, how much everyone starts with, and how to set the blinds.

Short answer: you only need a way to track numbers. Give everyone the same starting stack (1,000 is easy), play with blinds of 5 and 10, and keep score with a poker chips app, a written ledger, or small objects like coins. An app is the least work because it also handles the pot, side pots and whose turn it is.

How many chips should each player start with?

Without physical chips you can pick any numbers. What matters is the stack size compared with the big blind. Around 100 big blinds gives a relaxed game with room to play; around 50 makes it faster and more all-in heavy.

Running a hand with just cards and a phone

  1. Seat everyone and pick a dealer. The dealer button moves one seat to the left after every hand.
  2. Post the blinds. The player left of the button puts in the small blind, the next player the big blind.
  3. Deal two cards to each player, face down, starting left of the button.
  4. Betting round. Starting left of the big blind, each player folds, calls or raises. The app shows whose turn it is and the exact amount to call.
  5. The flop, turn and river. Burn one card, then deal three cards face up (the flop), then one (the turn), then one (the river). There's a betting round after each, starting left of the button.
  6. Showdown. Remaining players show their cards; the best five-card hand wins. If you're not sure who won, check with the who-wins calculator.

New to Texas Hold'em? Keep the hand rankings chart open on one phone for the first few rounds.

Settling up at the end

In a free-play game the biggest stack wins bragging rights. With real buy-ins, work out each player's result (what they cashed out minus what they bought in) and pay the difference. The settle-up calculator finds the fewest payments for you.

Questions

Can you play poker without chips at all?

Yes. Chips are only a way to keep score. Any method that tracks each player's stack and the pot works, and an app does it with no counting.

How many chips do you need for poker?

With an app, as many as you like, so start everyone with 1,000 and blinds of 5 and 10. A physical set for six players usually needs 300 to 500 chips in several colours.

Is it legal to play poker for money at home?

It depends on where you live. Many places allow private social games among friends, but rules vary widely. Free-play points are always fine.
